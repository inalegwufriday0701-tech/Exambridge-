KNOWLEDGE CENTRE

Explore Student Resources

Useful information organised around your academic needs.

Registration Essentials

Before registering for an examination, confirm that your personal information is accurate and consistent across your required documents.

  • Verify your personal information.
  • Research your preferred institution and course.
  • Confirm the correct subject combination.
  • Follow official registration announcements.

Examination Preparation

Effective preparation begins with a realistic study plan and familiarity with the examination format.

  • Study the current syllabus.
  • Practise CBT-style questions.
  • Understand examination centre regulations.
  • Confirm your examination schedule.

Admission Guidance

Admission processing may involve institutional recommendations and official approval procedures.

  • Understand your institution's admission requirements.
  • Monitor your official CAPS account.
  • Confirm your O-level information.
  • Follow institutional announcements.

Protect Your Information

Your examination credentials are confidential. Protect them throughout your admission journey.

  • Use only verified examination websites.
  • Never disclose passwords or OTPs.
  • Avoid unofficial payment requests.
  • Report suspicious messages.
